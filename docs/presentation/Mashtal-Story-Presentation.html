The real starting point

Meet the everyday struggle

A grower needs seedlings and advice. A nursery needs customers they can trust. A buyer wants quality without chasing five WhatsApp chats and a cousin’s recommendation.

  • Demand exists — but discovery is random
  • Trust is personal — not scalable
  • Knowledge is scattered — Google, neighbors, guesswork

The pain

“My plants are dying — and I don’t know who to ask, what to buy, or who to trust.”

Plant disease, irrigation mistakes, and bad product choices cost real money. Waiting days for advice is not a strategy.

Market reality

Three broken loops

  • Buyers can’t reliably find verified nurseries and products near them
  • Businesses struggle to sell online, manage orders, and look professional
  • Communities discuss farming in fragments — posts here, chats there, no storefront

The opportunity is not “another shop.” It’s a connected agricultural marketplace with trust and intelligence built in.

Insight

People don’t only need products.

They need relationships, proof, and answers — in the same place they buy.

Trust

Verified businesses, ratings, reviews

Access

Shop, chat, follow, save

Guidance

Community + AI plant help

The solution

Mashtal

One digital nursery floor for Lebanon’s agriculture ecosystem — where visitors discover, buy, learn, and talk… and businesses grow beyond the roadside shop.

How Mashtal solves it

From chaos to one journey

  • Discover trusted providers and community updates
  • Shop products with cart and secure checkout
  • Connect through posts, threads, follow, and chat
  • Decide faster with AI agronomy Q&A and leaf-photo analysis
  • Sell professionally with subscription, catalog, orders, analytics

For visitors & buyers

Find it. Trust it. Buy it.

Search & shop

Social proof

Follow businesses, reviews, verified badges

Checkout

Cart → Stripe payment → purchase history

For agricultural businesses

A digital storefront that works

  • Pay a subscription → unlock selling
  • Publish products, manage stock, receive orders
  • Post updates & join threads to stay visible
  • Chat with customers in realtime
  • See sales analytics that guide decisions

Intelligence layer

When the leaf looks wrong, Mashtal answers.

Ask farming questions — or upload a plant photo. Local disease analysis + treatment guidance turns panic into a next step… and can lead buyers back to the right products.

Product system

One brand. Four engines.

  • Marketplace — products, cart, payments
  • Community — posts, threads, follows, saves
  • Relationships — chat & Mashtal Support
  • Intelligence — AI assistant + disease model
